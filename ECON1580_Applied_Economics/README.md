# ECON 1580: Applied Economics — Theory and Practice

**University of the People**
**Credits**: 3

## Textbooks (both free, OpenStax)

- Shapiro et al. (2023). *Principles of macroeconomics* (3rd ed.). OpenStax. https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-macroeconomics-3e
- Shapiro et al. (2023). *Principles of microeconomics* (3rd ed.). OpenStax. https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-microeconomics-3e

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | Deliverables |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Economic Systems and Global Policy Impacts | **Discussion**, Assignment, Self-Quiz |
| 2 | Regulatory Policies and Market Competition | Assignment, Self-Quiz |
| 3 | Externalities, Public Goods, and Government Intervention | **Discussion**, Assignment, Self-Quiz, **Graded Quiz** |
| 4 | Environmental Policies and Sustainability | Assignment, Self-Quiz |
| 5 | Labor Markets, Wage Dynamics, and Economic Inequality | **Discussion**, Assignment, Self-Quiz |
| 6 | Fiscal and Monetary Policies | Assignment, Self-Quiz, **Graded Quiz** |
| 7 | Trade, Globalization, and Macroeconomic Perspectives | **Discussion**, Assignment, Self-Quiz |
| 8 | Financial Systems: Capital, Banking, Exchange, and Digital Innovations | Assignment, Self-Quiz |
| 9 | Final Exam | Review Quiz, Final Exam |
