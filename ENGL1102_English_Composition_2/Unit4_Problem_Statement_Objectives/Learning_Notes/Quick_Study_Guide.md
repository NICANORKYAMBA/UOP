## 🎯 Unit 4 at a Glance

### Three Core Components
1. **Problem Statement** - Identify the gap and justify the research
2. **Research Objectives** - Define SMART goals aligned to the problem
3. **Research Questions** - Frame specific inquiries linked to objectives

### The Research Flow
```
Literature Review (Unit 3)
    ↓ Gap Identification
Problem Statement (Unit 4)
    ↓ What needs addressing
Research Objectives (Unit 4)
    ↓ What the study aims to achieve
Research Questions (Unit 4)
    ↓ Specific inquiries to guide the study
Methodology (Unit 5+)
```

## 📊 Quick Reference Tables

### Problem Statement Formula

| Component | Purpose | Key Phrase |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| **Context** | Set the scene | "Cybersecurity threats continue to..." |
| **What We Know** | Existing research | "Research has established that..." |
| **The Gap** | What's missing | "However, limited research addresses..." |
| **Significance** | Why it matters | "This gap is critical because..." |
| **Direction** | What you'll do | "This study proposes..." |

---

### SMART Goals Checklist

| Letter | Criterion | Test Question | ✅ Example |
|--------|-----------|--------------|-----------|
| **S** | Specific | What exactly? | "Analyze phishing training effectiveness" |
| **M** | Measurable | How will I know? | "Compare breach rates with metrics" |
| **A** | Achievable | Can it be done? | "Review existing literature" (not "solve all cybersecurity") |
| **R** | Relevant | Does it connect? | Directly addresses the identified gap |
| **T** | Time-bound | By when? | "Literature from 2018-2024" |

---

### Goals vs. Objectives vs. Questions

| Aspect | Goal | Objective | Question |
|--------|------|-----------|----------|
| **Scope** | Broad | Specific | Precise |
| **Format** | Statement | "To + verb..." | "What/How...?" |
| **Number** | 1-2 | 3-5 | 1-2 per objective |
| **Verbs** | General | Bloom's action verbs | Question words |
| **Example** | "Improve small biz cybersecurity" | "To analyze training effectiveness" | "What training methods are most effective?" |

---

### Bloom's Taxonomy Verbs (Use These!)

| Level | Verbs | Use When... |
|-------|-------|------------|
| **Remember** | Define, list, identify | Establishing facts |
| **Understand** | Explain, summarize, classify | Describing concepts |
| **Apply** | Apply, demonstrate, implement | Using knowledge |
| **Analyze** | Analyze, compare, examine | Breaking down ideas |
| **Evaluate** | Evaluate, assess, justify | Making judgments |
| **Create** | Design, propose, develop | Building something new |

**Best for Research Objectives**: Analyze, Evaluate, Compare, Assess, Propose

---

### Types of Research Questions

| Type | Format | Example |
|------|--------|---------|
| **Descriptive** | "What are...?" | "What are the common threats?" |
| **Comparative** | "How does X compare to Y?" | "How do NIST and CISA compare?" |
| **Relationship** | "What is the relationship between...?" | "What links training to breach reduction?" |
| **Evaluative** | "To what extent...?" | "To what extent do frameworks address small biz?" |
| **Exploratory** | "What factors...?" | "What factors enable training sustainability?" |

## ✅ Assignment Checklist

### Problem Statement (Major Section)
- [ ] Context with data/citations
- [ ] Clear identification of the research gap
- [ ] Justification of significance
- [ ] Connection to existing literature (5 sources)
- [ ] Signal of research direction

### Research Objectives (Major Section)
- [ ] 1 general objective
- [ ] 3-4 specific objectives
- [ ] SMART criteria applied to each
- [ ] Bloom's action verbs used
- [ ] Aligned with problem statement

### Research Questions (Major Section)
- [ ] 3-5 specific questions
- [ ] Each linked to a specific objective
- [ ] Open-ended (not yes/no)
- [ ] Clear, focused, answerable
- [ ] Demonstrate critical analysis

### Formatting
- [ ] 500-750 words (body only)
- [ ] Times New Roman 12pt
- [ ] Double-spaced
- [ ] APA citations and references
- [ ] Credible, relevant sources

## 🔑 Key Formulas

### Problem Statement Formula
```
CONTEXT + EXISTING KNOWLEDGE + GAP + SIGNIFICANCE = Problem Statement
```

### Objective Formula
```
To + [Bloom's verb] + [specific what] + [specific context/scope]
```

### Question Formula
```
[Question word] + [specific focus] + [context] + [scope]?
```
