## 📝 Assignment Requirements

### Written Assignment: Problem Statement, Objectives & Research Questions

**Task**: Based on literature review and gap identification from Units 2-3, craft:

1. **Problem Statement** (Significant portion)
   - Describe the issue or gap in knowledge
   - Justify the importance of addressing this problem

2. **Research Objectives** (Well-defined)
   - Define the overall goals of the research
   - Concise and directly address the problem statement

3. **Research Questions** (Specific and focused)
   - Frame specific inquiries to achieve the objectives
   - Clear, focused, and answerable through research
   - Link each question to a specific objective

### Formatting Requirements
- **Word Count**: 500-750 words (excluding title and references)
- **Font**: Times New Roman, 12pt
- **Spacing**: Double-spaced
- **Citations**: APA format with in-text citations
- **Sources**: High-quality, credible, relevant
