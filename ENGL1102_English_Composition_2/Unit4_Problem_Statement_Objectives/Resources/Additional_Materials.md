## 📝 Key Terms Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Problem Statement** | A concise description of the research gap and its significance |
| **Research Gap** | An area where knowledge is incomplete or missing |
| **Research Objective** | A clear statement of what the research aims to achieve |
| **General Objective** | The overarching aim of the study |
| **Specific Objective** | A focused, measurable step toward the general objective |
| **Research Question** | A specific inquiry the study seeks to answer |
| **SMART Goals** | Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound |
| **Bloom's Taxonomy** | Classification of cognitive skills from basic recall to creative synthesis |
| **Operationalize** | Define abstract concepts in measurable terms |
| **Hypothesis** | A testable prediction about the relationship between variables |
| **Literature Review** | Systematic analysis of existing research on a topic |
| **Synthesis** | Combining information from multiple sources for new insights |
