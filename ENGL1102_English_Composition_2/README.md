## 📖 Course Description

ENGL1102 continues the study of writing skills in English from ENGL0008. This course builds on foundational writing skills, enabling students to write advanced essays and research papers while maintaining academic writing conventions. The course focuses on:

- Formulating and substantiating arguments proficiently
- Analytical reasoning and issue resolution
- Writing a complete research paper by the end of the course
- Pre-writing and recursive writing techniques
- Synthesis and paraphrasing

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. Understand and demonstrate argumentative, descriptive, narrative, expositive and persuasive writing
2. Apply the scientific method of inquiry
3. Demonstrate knowledge of individual research processes
4. Understand the ethics of research work
5. Use the recursive nature of writing
6. Apply the art of paraphrasing
7. Demonstrate the art of synthesis in writing
8. Examine and analyze texts for literature review and data
9. Demonstrate basic knowledge of research theories
10. Demonstrate awareness of basic skills of English grammar
11. Master common terminology associated with research design
