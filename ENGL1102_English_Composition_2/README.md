## Course Description

This course builds on English Composition 1, focusing on research, argumentation, and advanced writing techniques. Students will develop critical thinking and analytical writing skills through research-based essays.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:
- Conduct effective academic research
- Evaluate and synthesize sources critically
- Construct well-reasoned arguments
- Write clear, organized, and persuasive essays
- Use proper citation formats (APA/MLA)
- Revise and edit work effectively
- Engage in peer review constructively

---

## Writing Standards

- Follow APA 7th Edition formatting (unless specified otherwise)
- Proper in-text citations and reference lists
- Clear thesis statements
- Well-organized paragraphs with topic sentences
- Academic tone and style
- Proofread for grammar, spelling, and punctuation

---

## Notes

- All essays will be properly cited
- Use credible academic sources
- Follow word count requirements
- Submit drafts for peer review when required
