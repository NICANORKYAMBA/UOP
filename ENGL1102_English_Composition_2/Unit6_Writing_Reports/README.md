# ENGL1102 Unit 6 — Writing Reports

**Course**: ENGL1102 English Composition 2  
**Unit**: 6 — Writing Reports  

---

## Learning Objectives

- Present and interpret research findings effectively
- Analyze data using appropriate techniques and tools
- Write a structured formal report based on quantitative data

---

## Topics Covered

- Research discussions and findings
- Analyzing charts and quantitative data
- Writing formal reports
- Interpreting pie chart data (BLS American Time Use Survey)

---

## Key Readings

1. Serdikoff, S. (2021). *Research methods for the behavioral sciences* (5th ed.). GALILEO Open Learning Materials. https://oer.galileo.usg.edu/psychology-textbooks/12/
   - Section 16 — Analyzing the Data
   - Section 17 — Drawing Conclusions and Reporting the Results

2. College of Business and Economics. (2024, November 1). *Guidelines for reports*. Boise State University. https://www.boisestate.edu/cobe/cobe-writing-style-guide/guidelines-for-reports/
