# Unit 6 Quick Study Guide — Writing Reports

**Course**: ENGL1102 English Composition 2

---

## The Core Task

Read a pie chart → interpret the data → write a structured report → cite your sources.

---

## Report Structure at a Glance

```
Title
Introduction    → What is this report about? What data source?
Findings        → What does the chart show? (specific %)
Analysis        → What do the findings mean? Why do they matter?
Conclusion      → Key takeaway + significance
References      → APA format
```

---

## Serdikoff (2021) Key Points

| Section | Key Idea |
|---|---|
| Section 16 | Analysis transforms raw data into interpretable findings |
| Section 17 | Conclusions must stay within the scope of the evidence — no overgeneralizing |

---

## Chart Analysis Checklist

- [ ] Identified the largest segment and its percentage
- [ ] Identified the smallest segment and its percentage
- [ ] Grouped related categories where relevant
- [ ] Compared at least two segments
- [ ] Interpreted what the pattern means (not just described it)
- [ ] Noted at least one limitation of the data
- [ ] Cited the BLS source for all data points
