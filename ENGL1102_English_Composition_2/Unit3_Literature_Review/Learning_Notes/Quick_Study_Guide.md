## 📊 Quick Reference Tables

### CRAAP Test for Source Evaluation

| Letter | Criterion | Key Questions |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| **C** | Currency | Is it current? When published? |
| **R** | Relevance | Does it relate to my topic? |
| **A** | Authority | Who wrote it? Are they qualified? |
| **A** | Accuracy | Is it supported by evidence? |
| **P** | Purpose | Why was it written? Bias? |

---

### Paraphrasing vs. Quoting vs. Summarizing

| Aspect | Paraphrase | Quote | Summary |
|--------|------------|-------|---------|
| **Length** | Similar | Exact | Shorter |
| **Words** | Your own | Author's exact | Your own |
| **Use** | Specific ideas | Powerful language | Overview |
| **Citation** | Required | Required | Required |

---

### Types of Research Gaps

| Gap Type | Definition | Example |
|----------|------------|---------|
| **Knowledge** | Unstudied topic | "No research on X" |
| **Methodological** | Different methods needed | "Only surveys used" |
| **Population** | Understudied group | "Focus on large firms only" |
| **Practical** | Theory vs. practice | "Hard to implement" |
| **Empirical** | Lack of data | "No measurements exist" |
| **Geographical** | Limited regions | "Only Western studies" |

## ✅ Assignment Checklist

### Part 1: Source Summaries (25 points)
- [ ] Summary of Source 1 with quote AND paraphrase
- [ ] Summary of Source 2 with quote AND paraphrase
- [ ] Summary of Source 3 with quote AND paraphrase
- [ ] Summary of Source 4 with quote AND paraphrase
- [ ] Summary of Source 5 with quote AND paraphrase

### Part 2: Synthesis (20 points)
- [ ] 1-2 paragraphs synthesizing sources
- [ ] Organized by themes (not source-by-source)
- [ ] Shows relationships between sources
- [ ] Includes analysis (not just summary)
- [ ] Uses transition words

### Part 3: Gap Identification (15 points)
- [ ] Identifies specific gap in literature
- [ ] Explains how to address gap OR innovation to bring
- [ ] Supported by source citations
- [ ] Clear and specific

### Formatting (10 points)
- [ ] 500-750 words (body only)
- [ ] Times New Roman 12pt
- [ ] Double-spaced
- [ ] 1" margins
- [ ] APA citations and references

## 🔑 Key Formulas

### Paraphrasing Formula
1. Read and understand
2. Set aside original
3. Write in your own words
4. Check against original
5. Cite the source

### Synthesis Formula
1. Identify common themes
2. Organize by theme (not by source)
3. Show relationships between sources
4. Add your analysis
5. Build toward conclusion

### Gap Statement Formula
> While [existing research] has established [what we know], [specific gap] remains unexplored.

## 💡 Quick Tips

### For Analyzing Sources
- Use CRAAP test
- Check author credentials
- Verify publication venue
- Look for peer review
- Assess bias

### For Paraphrasing
- Don't look at original while writing
- Change sentence structure
- Use different vocabulary
- Keep technical terms
- Always cite

### For Synthesizing
- Group by themes, not sources
- Use transition words (however, similarly, moreover)
- Show agreements AND disagreements
- Add your own analysis
- Don't just list sources

### For Finding Gaps
- Read "future research" sections
- Notice what's missing
- Look for contradictions
- Consider dated research
- Think about practical limitations

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Analyzing
- ❌ Using unreliable sources (blogs, Wikipedia)
- ❌ Not checking publication dates
- ❌ Ignoring author credentials

### Paraphrasing
- ❌ Patchwriting (changing few words)
- ❌ Forgetting to cite
- ❌ Changing the meaning
- ❌ Copying phrases

### Synthesizing
- ❌ Organizing source-by-source
- ❌ Just summarizing (no analysis)
- ❌ Ignoring contradictions
- ❌ No transitions

### Gap Identification
- ❌ Claiming gaps that don't exist
- ❌ Being too vague
- ❌ Identifying gaps you can't address

## 📝 Transition Words for Synthesis

### Agreement
- Similarly, likewise, in addition, furthermore, also, moreover

### Disagreement
- However, in contrast, conversely, on the other hand, nevertheless

### Extension
- Moreover, additionally, building on this, furthermore

### Causation
- Therefore, consequently, as a result, thus, hence
