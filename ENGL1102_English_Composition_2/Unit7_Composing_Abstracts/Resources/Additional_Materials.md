## Key Skills for This Unit

- Identifying the six elements of an effective abstract
- Condensing objectives and questions without losing specificity
- Writing methodology in 2–3 sentences
- Avoiding in-text citations within the abstract body
- Matching tense to content type (present for facts, past for what was done)
