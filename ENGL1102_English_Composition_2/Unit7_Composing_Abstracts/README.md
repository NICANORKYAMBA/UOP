# ENGL1102 Unit 7 — Composing Abstracts

## Learning Objective

Compose an effective abstract summarizing the key aspects of your research.

1. Serdikoff, S. (2021). *Research methods for the behavioral sciences* (5th ed.), pp. 121–122. GALILEO Open Learning Materials. https://oer.galileo.usg.edu/psychology-textbooks/12/

2. Writing Center, University of Wisconsin–Madison. (n.d.). *Writing an abstract for your research paper*. https://writing.wisc.edu/handbook/assignments/writing-an-abstract-for-your-research-paper/
