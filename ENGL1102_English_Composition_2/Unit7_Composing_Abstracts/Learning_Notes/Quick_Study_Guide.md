# Unit 7 Quick Study Guide — Composing Abstracts

**Course**: ENGL1102 English Composition 2

---

## Abstract in One Formula

```
[Background: why this matters]
+ [Problem: what gap exists]
+ [Objectives: what the study aims to do]
+ [Questions: what it asks]
+ [Method: how it investigates]
+ [Findings/Proposal: what it concludes]
= Effective Abstract (250–500 words)
```

---

## Six-Element Checklist

- [ ] Background/context — significance of the topic
- [ ] Problem statement — the specific gap being addressed
- [ ] Research objectives — what the study aims to achieve (use action verbs: analyze, evaluate, identify, propose)
- [ ] Research questions — the specific questions guiding the study
- [ ] Methodology — research design and approach
- [ ] Main findings or proposed contribution

---

## What NOT to Include

- In-text citations
- Tables or figures
- Undefined abbreviations
- Information not in the paper
- Excessive background detail

---

## Tense Guide

| Content | Tense |
|---|---|
| General facts, significance | Present |
| What the study did | Past |
| What the study proposes/concludes | Present |
