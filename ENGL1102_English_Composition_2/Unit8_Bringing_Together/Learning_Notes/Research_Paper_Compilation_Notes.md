## 1. Compiling the Research Paper

A complete APA research paper includes these sections in order:

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Title Page | Author, course, institution, date |
| Abstract | 150–500 word self-contained summary |
| Introduction | Context, significance, paper roadmap |
| Background | Broader context and prior knowledge |
| Problem Statement | Specific gap the study addresses |
| Objectives & Questions | What the study aims to do and ask |
| Literature Review | Synthesis of existing sources |
| Methodology | How the study is conducted |
| Results & Discussion | What was found and what it means |
| Conclusion | Summary, implications, future research |
| References | APA 7th edition list |

## 2. Editing vs. Proofreading

| Stage | Focus | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Revising** | Content, argument, structure, coherence | Before editing |
| **Editing** | Clarity, conciseness, word choice, transitions | After revising |
| **Proofreading** | Spelling, grammar, punctuation, formatting | Last step |

Weaver et al. (2016) emphasize that revising addresses *what* you say; editing addresses *how* you say it (Section 2.4).

---

## 3. Editing Checklist (Weaver et al., 2016, Section 2.4)

- [ ] Every paragraph has a clear topic sentence
- [ ] Transitions connect paragraphs logically
- [ ] No unnecessary repetition of ideas
- [ ] Active voice used where possible
- [ ] Precise word choices — no vague language
- [ ] Each section flows into the next

---

## 4. Proofreading Strategies (Hall & Wallace, 2018)

- Read aloud — catches awkward phrasing your eye skips
- Read backwards sentence by sentence — isolates grammar errors
- Print and mark — different medium reveals different errors
- Check one error type at a time (commas, then apostrophes, etc.)
- Use a grammar tracking log (Hall & Wallace, 2018, Section 5.4)

---

## 5. Writing a Strong Conclusion (Hall & Wallace, 2018, Section 4.7)

A conclusion must:
1. Restate the thesis/main argument (not copy it)
2. Summarize key findings without introducing new information
3. State the broader significance — why does this matter?
4. Suggest directions for future research

Avoid: "In conclusion, this paper has shown..." — too formulaic.

---

## 6. APA Formatting Reminders (Serdikoff, 2021, Section 58–59)

- Double-spaced throughout, including references
- Times New Roman 12pt, 1" margins
- Running head on title page only (student papers)
- Level 1 headings: centered, bold
- Level 2 headings: left-aligned, bold
- In-text citations: (Author, Year, p. X) for direct quotes
- References: hanging indent, alphabetical order
