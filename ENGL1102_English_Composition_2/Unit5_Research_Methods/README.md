## Assignment Summary

### Discussion Assignment (200–350 words)
Draft a professional email to a potential advisor or collaborator explaining your research idea and seeking feedback. The email must:
- Identify which research methodology (qualitative, quantitative, or mixed-methods) best suits the study
- Discuss why this approach is most appropriate for the research goals
- Demonstrate clarity, tone, and conciseness

**Rubric Weights**:
- Drafting Emails (3 pts): Well-developed, identifies and justifies research method
- Connection to Course Materials (1 pt): Strong citations and references
- Peer Feedback (2 pts): Minimum 2 constructive responses
- Clarity and Mechanics (1 pt): Polished, error-free
- Timeliness (2 pts): Initial post by Sunday
- Word Count (1 pt): 200–350 words

**Total**: 10 points
