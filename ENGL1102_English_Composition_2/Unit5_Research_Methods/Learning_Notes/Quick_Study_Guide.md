# Unit 5 Quick Study Guide: Research Methods & Professional Email

**Course**: ENGL1102 English Composition 2

---

## Research Methods at a Glance

| | Qualitative | Quantitative | Mixed-Methods |
|--|-------------|--------------|---------------|
| **Asks** | Why? How? | What? How much? | Both |
| **Data** | Words, themes | Numbers, stats | Both |
| **Sample** | Small, purposive | Large, random | Both |
| **Goal** | Explore/understand | Measure/test | Comprehensive |
| **Example** | Interviews, case studies | Surveys, experiments | Sequential/concurrent |

---

## Choosing a Methodology — Decision Guide

1. Is your question about *experiences, meanings, or processes*? → **Qualitative**
2. Is your question about *frequency, correlation, or causation*? → **Quantitative**
3. Do you need *both depth and breadth*? → **Mixed-Methods**

---

## Professional Email Checklist

- [ ] Clear, specific subject line
- [ ] Formal salutation (Dear Dr./Professor)
- [ ] Self-introduction (name, program, institution)
- [ ] Clear statement of research idea
- [ ] Research methodology identified and justified
- [ ] Specific, single request
- [ ] Formal closing and full signature
- [ ] 200–350 words
- [ ] Proofread for grammar and spelling

---

## Discussion Assignment Requirements

- 200–350 words (body only, excluding references)
- Draft a professional email to an advisor/collaborator
- Identify research methodology (qualitative/quantitative/mixed)
- Justify why that methodology fits the research goals
- APA citations from course readings
- Post by Sunday; reply to 2 peers by Wednesday
