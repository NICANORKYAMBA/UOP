# ENGL1102 Course Overview & Quick Reference

## 📋 Quick Facts

- **Course**: English Composition 2
- **Credits**: 3
- **Duration**: 9 weeks (8 units + final exam)
- **Prerequisites**: UNIV1001 and ENGL0008

## 🎯 What You'll Learn

This course teaches you to write **advanced academic essays and research papers**. By the end, you'll complete a full research paper using:

- Argumentative writing
- Research methodology
- Source evaluation and citation
- Synthesis and paraphrasing
- Academic conventions (APA format)

## 📊 Grade Distribution

| Component | Weight | Details |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Discussions | 40% | 4 forums (Units 1, 2, 5, 7) |
| Assignments | 40% | 5 assignments (Units 2, 3, 4, 6, 8) |
| Graded Quizzes | 10% | 2 quizzes (Units 3, 6) |
| Final Exam | 10% | Unit 9 (comprehensive) |

## 🎯 Key Skills to Master

1. **Argumentative Writing**: Make and support claims
2. **Research**: Find, evaluate, cite sources
3. **Synthesis**: Combine multiple sources
4. **Paraphrasing**: Restate ideas in your own words
5. **Grammar**: Maintain proper English conventions
6. **APA Format**: Follow academic citation style
7. **Critical Thinking**: Analyze and evaluate information

## 📝 Writing Process

1. **Pre-writing**: Brainstorm, research, outline
2. **Drafting**: Write first draft
3. **Revising**: Improve content and organization
4. **Editing**: Fix grammar, spelling, punctuation
5. **Proofreading**: Final check before submission

## 🚫 Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Submitting late (no make-ups usually)
- Plagiarism (zero tolerance)
- Shallow discussion responses
- Not citing sources
- Ignoring rubrics
- Procrastinating on assignments
- Skipping Self-Quizzes (they help you learn!)

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Use the textbooks** - They're free and comprehensive
2. **Start early** - Writing takes time
3. **Seek feedback** - Learn from peer responses
4. **Practice grammar** - Review regularly
5. **Build progressively** - Each unit builds on previous
6. **Ask questions** - Use Course Forum
7. **Stay organized** - Keep track of deadlines
