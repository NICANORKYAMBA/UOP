Peer Response Templates for Discussion Forum
(Minimum 75 words each, 3-4 complete sentences)

PEER RESPONSE #1 TEMPLATE:
---
Hi [Classmate Name],

I found your analysis of [specific point they made] particularly insightful, especially regarding [specific detail]. Your emphasis on [another point] aligns well with what Gupta and Goyal (2020) discuss about [relevant concept]. I'm curious about your perspective on [related question or extension of their idea]. How do you think [specific consideration] might impact the decision-making process you described?

[Your Name]
---

PEER RESPONSE #2 TEMPLATE:
---
Hello [Classmate Name],

Your discussion of [specific topic] provided an interesting perspective that I hadn't fully considered. I particularly appreciated how you connected [concept A] to [concept B] in the context of business needs. Building on your point about [specific detail], I wonder if [additional consideration or alternative viewpoint]. Do you think [follow-up question related to their post] would be a significant factor in the scenarios you described?

[Your Name]
---

EXAMPLE PEER RESPONSE #1:
---
Hi Sarah,

I found your analysis of workstation requirements for graphic designers particularly insightful, especially regarding the emphasis on dedicated GPUs and high RAM capacity. Your emphasis on balancing cost with performance aligns well with what Gupta and Goyal (2020) discuss about computer classification based on purpose and capability. I'm curious about your perspective on whether cloud-based rendering solutions might reduce the need for high-end local workstations. How do you think network bandwidth limitations might impact the decision-making process you described for creative roles?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
---

EXAMPLE PEER RESPONSE #2:
---
Hello Michael,

Your discussion of Von Neumann architecture's impact on system selection provided an interesting perspective that I hadn't fully considered. I particularly appreciated how you connected the memory bottleneck concept to the importance of SSD selection in modern business environments. Building on your point about the shared bus limitation, I wonder if emerging technologies like non-volatile memory express (NVMe) drives might further address these architectural constraints. Do you think the fundamental Von Neumann bottleneck would be a significant factor when choosing between different storage technologies for the scenarios you described?

Regards,
[Your Name]
---

TIPS FOR WRITING PEER RESPONSES:
1. Reference specific points from their post (shows you read carefully)
2. Connect to course materials/readings when possible
3. Add value by extending their ideas or offering alternative perspectives
4. Ask a thoughtful question that encourages further discussion
5. Be respectful and constructive
6. Aim for 75-100 words to exceed minimum requirements
7. Use proper grammar and professional tone
