## 📝 PRACTICE QUESTIONS

After studying, test yourself with these questions:

1. What are the four core functions of an OS?
2. Differentiate between batch and time-sharing OS.
3. What is the difference between hard and soft real-time systems?
4. Compare Android and iOS in terms of updates and customization.
5. Explain virtual memory and its importance.
6. What are the key features of mobile operating systems?
7. Describe the evolution of OS from 1960s to present.
8. What is process scheduling and why is it important?
9. How does app sandboxing improve mobile security?
10. What are the advantages of distributed operating systems?
