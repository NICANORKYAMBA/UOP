# CS 2204 Self-Quiz Unit 2: Answers and Reasons

| Q | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | It allows multiple signals to share the same transmission medium | That is the definition of multiplexing (FDM, TDM, WDM) |
| 2 | Susceptibility to eavesdropping | Security is the concern; fiber is hard to tap, wireless is easy to intercept |
| 3 | FALSE | Error detection and correction (CRC, checksums) belong to the data link and transport layers; the physical layer only sends bits |
| 4 | Physical pathways | Guided = cables (twisted pair, coax, fiber); unguided = radio, microwave, infrared |
| 5 | Guided media signals travel through physical pathways, while unguided media signals travel through the air | Same distinction as Q4 |
