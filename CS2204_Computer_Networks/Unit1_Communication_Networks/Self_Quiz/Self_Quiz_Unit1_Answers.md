| Q | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Network Access Layer | The bottom TCP/IP layer covers the OSI physical and data link layers: MAC (physical) addresses and access to the medium |
| 2 | Session Layer | OSI layer 5 sets up, manages and ends sessions between applications |
| 3 | TRUE | A modem modulates digital data onto an analog carrier wave (and demodulates it back) |
| 4 | They can have an infinite number of values within a range | Analog signals are continuous; digital signals are discrete |
| 5 | Ethernet | The most common wired LAN (Dordal, 2023, Section 1.9); Bluetooth and Zigbee are wireless PAN, Wi-Fi is wireless LAN |
