🔑 KEY CONCEPTS TO MASTER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MUST KNOW:
⭐ Star topology = best for small business
⭐ Full-duplex = modern standard
⭐ Switch = Layer 2, Router = Layer 3
⭐ DNS translates domain names to IP addresses
⭐ CIA Triad = Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability
⭐ Virus needs host, Worm self-replicates, Trojan disguises
⭐ Firewall = network security barrier
⭐ Defense-in-depth = multiple security layers

UNDERSTAND WHY:
- Why star topology better than bus/ring
- Why full-duplex better than half-duplex
- Why switch better than hub
- Why router needed for Internet connection
- Why encryption important for confidentiality
- Why backups important for availability
- Why multiple security layers needed
