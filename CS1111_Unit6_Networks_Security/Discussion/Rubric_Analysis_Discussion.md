HOW THE DISCUSSION POST ACHIEVES LEVEL 4:

✓ THOROUGH EXPLANATION OF ALL THREE MODES:

SIMPLEX:
- Definition: "One-direction flow only, from sender to receiver"
- Characteristic: "No reverse communication capability"
- General example: Television broadcasting
- Business scenarios:
  * Security camera systems → video feeds to recording devices
  * Digital signage → receiving content from central servers
- When appropriate: "Bidirectional communication is unnecessary and cost reduction is prioritized"

HALF-DUPLEX:
- Definition: "Bidirectional communication, but only one direction at a time"
- Characteristic: "Devices take turns"
- General example: Walkie-talkies (perfect demonstration)
- Business scenarios:
  * Older Ethernet with hubs → collision detection mechanisms
  * Wireless networks → shared radio frequency mediums
- Why still relevant: "Necessitate turn-taking to avoid interference"

FULL-DUPLEX:
- Definition: "Simultaneous bidirectional communication"
- Characteristic: "Concurrent sending and receiving without conflicts"
- Mechanism: "Dedicated transmit and receive wire pairs"
- Benefit: "Doubles available bandwidth and eliminates delays"
- Business scenarios (MULTIPLE):
  * VoIP phone systems → simultaneous audio transmission/reception
  * Video conferencing → transmitting local while receiving remote streams
  * Database servers → handling simultaneous queries
  * File servers → concurrent uploads and downloads
- Recommendation: "Should be standard for all wired connections"

✓ INSIGHTFUL EXAMPLES:
  - Not just listing modes, but explaining WHY each is used
  - Specific business applications for each mode
  - Performance implications explained
  - Cost considerations mentioned
  - Technical reasons provided (e.g., "separate paths eliminate collisions")

✓ CLEARLY ILLUSTRATE APPLICABILITY:
  - Simplex: When feedback not needed, cost priority
  - Half-duplex: When medium is shared (wireless), technical limitation
  - Full-duplex: When performance critical, modern standard
  - Each scenario clearly matched to appropriate mode

✓ PRACTICAL SCENARIOS:
  - Security cameras (simplex) - real business need
  - Wireless networks (half-duplex) - common in businesses

  - Video conferencing (full-duplex) - remote work trend
