EXAMPLE PEER RESPONSE #3: Transmission Modes Focus
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Hi Jennifer,

Your analysis of full-duplex transmission advantages for modern business networks is spot-on, particularly your emphasis on simultaneous bidirectional communication eliminating wait times. I agree that full-duplex is essential for VoIP and video conferencing applications. Building on your discussion, Borodin (2024) explains that full-duplex effectively doubles bandwidth by using separate transmit and receive channels.

One practical consideration I'd add is ensuring all network components support full-duplex operation. Older devices or improperly configured switches might default to half-duplex, creating performance bottlenecks. This is particularly relevant when businesses upgrade incrementally rather than replacing entire infrastructure simultaneously.

Given your focus on real-time communication applications, how would you prioritize network traffic when bandwidth becomes constrained? Would Quality of Service (QoS) configuration on managed switches be worth the additional complexity for a small business?
