Great post! You explained network fundamentals clearly, making complex concepts accessible for business owners. I agree that LAN with star topology is ideal for small businesses due to its reliability and manageability.

Regarding your discussion question about securing a growing network, small businesses should implement several simple yet effective steps. First, enable WPA3 encryption on wireless networks and use strong, unique passwords changed regularly (Chauhan & Jangra, 2020). Second, install and maintain a business-grade firewall to control incoming and outgoing traffic, blocking unauthorized access attempts. Third, implement network segmentation separating guest Wi-Fi from business operations, preventing visitors from accessing sensitive data (Borodin, 2024). Fourth, enable automatic security updates on all network devices including routers and switches. Fifth, establish basic access controls ensuring employees only access necessary network resources. Finally, conduct regular backups stored off-site protecting against data loss from hardware failures or cyberattacks.

These steps are affordable, don't require extensive technical expertise, and provide strong foundational security as the business scales. Your emphasis on full-duplex transmission is particularly relevant since it supports the bandwidth demands of modern security tools like video surveillance and encrypted communications.
