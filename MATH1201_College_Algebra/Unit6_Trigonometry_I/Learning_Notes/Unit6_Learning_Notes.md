## 1. Angles
- Degrees to radians: multiply by π/180. Radians to degrees: multiply by 180/π.
- Coterminal angles differ by multiples of 360° (2π). Reference angle: the acute angle to the
  x-axis (QII: 180° - θ, QIII: θ - 180°, QIV: 360° - θ).

## 2. Right-triangle ratios (SOH CAH TOA)
sin = opposite/hypotenuse, cos = adjacent/hypotenuse, tan = opposite/adjacent;
csc = 1/sin, sec = 1/cos, cot = 1/tan. Angle of elevation: looking up from the horizontal.

## 3. Unit circle
Point (x, y) at angle θ: cos θ = x, sin θ = y, tan θ = y/x. Signs: QI all positive, QII sin,
QIII tan, QIV cos ("All Students Take Calculus").

## 4. Graphs
- y = A sin(Bx - C) + D: amplitude |A|, period 2π/|B|, phase shift C/B, midline y = D.
- y = A tan(Bx - C) + D: stretch |A|, period π/|B|, asymptotes where Bx - C = π/2 + kπ.
- cos is even (cos(-x) = cos x); sin and tan are odd.

## 5. Inverse trig functions
| Function | Domain | Range |
|---|---|---|
| arcsin | [-1, 1] | [-π/2, π/2] |
| arccos | [-1, 1] | [0, π] |
| arctan | all reals | (-π/2, π/2) |
