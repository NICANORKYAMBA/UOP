# PHIL 1404 Self-Quiz Unit 2: Answers and Reasons

All answers come from Morgan (2019), Chapter 2 of the course textbook.

| Q | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Issues arising from interpreting ancient commands in modern contexts | The chapter asks how to apply ancient commands to cloning, assisted suicide or nuclear weapons |
| 2 | Moral obligations are discerned through human reason | Natural law: purposes in nature are discovered by reason |
| 3 | Whether X is pious because the gods approve of X, or whether the gods approve of X because X is pious | The chapter's statement of the Euthyphro dilemma (Box C) |
| 4 | Thomas Aquinas | "The moral theology of Thomas Aquinas contains one influential version of the NLT" |
| 5 | Divine Command Theory | DCT: X is obligatory if and only if God commands X |
