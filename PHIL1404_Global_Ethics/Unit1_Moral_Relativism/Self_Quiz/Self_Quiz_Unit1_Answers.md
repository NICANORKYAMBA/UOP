# PHIL 1404 Self-Quiz Unit 1: Answers and Reasons

Ungraded, unlimited attempts. All five answers come straight from Rezkalla (2019), Chapter 1 of the course textbook.

| Q | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | No, not if it is widely accepted in a culture | For the relativist, a practice is right if the culture accepts it; the chapter lists this as one of relativism's troubling consequences ("Some Things Just Seem Wrong") |
| 2 | A movement calling for the abolition of slavery | The chapter's "Relative to Whom?" example: anti-slavery Americans who went against the majority view |
| 3 | Informative ethics | The three branches are metaethics, normative ethics and applied ethics (McCombs video) |
| 4 | Culture is king. | Herodotus, *Histories* 3.38.4, quoted in the Metaethical Relativism section |
| 5 | TRUE relative to the standpoint of the person or culture that has the belief. | The chapter's own definition of metaethical relativism |
