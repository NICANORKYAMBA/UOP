# PHIL 1404 Unit 1 Learning Notes: Are Right and Wrong Matters of Opinion?

Main reading: Rezkalla (2019), Chapter 1 of Matthews and Hendricks, *Introduction to Philosophy: Ethics*. The quiz questions mostly come from this chapter.

## Branches of ethics (McCombs School of Business, 2018)
- **Metaethics:** what morality is and whether moral claims can be true.
- **Normative ethics:** frameworks for deciding what we ought to do (later units: virtue ethics, utilitarianism, deontology, social contract).
- **Applied ethics:** real issues such as euthanasia, business ethics, technology.

## Realism vs anti-realism
- **Moral realism:** there are mind-independent moral facts that are true even if people believe otherwise (rape is wrong whatever anyone thinks).
- **Moral anti-realism:** no mind-independent moral facts. Relativism is one form of anti-realism.

## Three kinds of relativism
| Kind | Claim | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Descriptive | Moral codes differ across cultures, and we can observe it | Mildest, a fact of anthropology |
| Metaethical | Moral claims are true or false only relative to the person or culture holding them | Stronger, about the nature of moral truth |
| Normative | No culture should judge other cultures' codes as inferior or interfere with them | Strongest |

- Herodotus: "Culture is king" (*Histories* 3.38.4).
- Example in the chapter: sati (suttee), widow burning in parts of India, outlawed by the British in 1829.
- Subjectivism: morality is relative to each individual instead of each culture.

## The problem of moral diversity: realist replies
1. Many disagreements are about non-moral facts (senicide example: killing elders so they keep useful bodies in the afterlife, based on care).
2. Disagreement does not mean there is no right answer (quadratic equation example).

## Objections to relativism
- **Relative to whom?** "Culture" is vague. Were the abolitionists wrong because they were a minority? How many people make a culture?
- **Some things just seem wrong:** relativism must accept sati, FGM, infanticide and slavery wherever a culture accepts them.
- **Tolerance problem:** saying everyone should be tolerant is itself a universal value, which relativism denies. Some things (child abuse, slavery) should not be tolerated.
- **No moral progress:** abolishing slavery or giving women the vote becomes mere change, not improvement (Wilberforce, Martin Luther King Jr.).
- Only 27.7% of professional philosophers were anti-realists in a 2009 survey.

## Pluralism is not relativism (McCombs School of Business, 2019)
Pluralism means many viewpoints exist in one society (diversity). Relativism says no view is more correct than another.

## Sikka (2012)
Cultural norms are often shaped by those in power, so "the culture's morality" can hide the interests of elites.
